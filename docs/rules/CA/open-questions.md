# California: open questions

Each question is tagged with what answers it:

- **Law:** needs an attorney.
- **Terms:** answered by the account's own terms, supplied as facts.
- **Policy:** an institution choice. It may be stricter than the statute, never looser.

Until a question is answered, the affected cases are **not determinable** (or use the stated conservative default). The research notes summarize [legal-review.md](legal-review.md), which has the quotes and sources.

| Q2 | With several affiants on Path B, how is payment split? | Law (with Policy) | Path B shares | One payment to all affiants jointly, or shares only as the affidavit states them. | The affiants "are entitled to have the property ... paid ... to them" (§ 13105(a)(1)); the successor is "all of the beneficiaries" (§ 13006). | Q3 | Day counting for "40 days have elapsed" (§ 13100): is the earliest payment the death date + 40 or + 41? Does CCP § 12a (holiday extension) apply to a waiting period? | Law | Path B earliest payment | **Death date + 41** (conservative), with no holiday extension; the affidavit is signed on or after that date. | CCP § 12, Civ. Code § 10 and Gov. Code § 6800 exclude the first day and include the last, so day 40 is death + 40. Whether 40 days "have elapsed" on day 40 or only after it ends is unsettled in California; no case was located. Washington's Supreme Court requires full days (*Troxell*), which is persuasive but not binding here. |
