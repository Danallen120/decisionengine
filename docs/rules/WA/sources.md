**Cases** (full text from the Caselaw Access Project; none citator-checked):

- **Day counting:** *Troxell v. Rainier Pub. Sch. Dist. No. 307*, 154 Wn.2d 345, 111 P.3d 1173 (2005); *Christensen v. Ellsworth*, 162 Wn.2d 365 (2007).
- **Superwill and designations:** *In re Estate of Burks*, 124 Wn. App. 327, 100 P.3d 328 (2004); *Manary v. Anderson*, 176 Wn.2d 342 (2013); *Dahle v. Nadolski*, 127 Wn. App. 783 (2005).
- **Contract of deposit, setoff, and interpleader:** *Estate of Brownfield v. Bank of America*, 170 Wn. App. 553 (2012); *Kalk v. Security Pacific Bank Washington*, 126 Wn.2d 346, 894 P.2d 559 (1995); *Kitsap Bank v. Denley*, 177 Wn. App. 559 (2013).
- **Community property agreements:** *Johnson v. Bachmeier*, 147 Wn.2d 60 (2002); *Higgins v. Stafford*, 123 Wn.2d 160, 866 P.2d 31 (1994); *Lyon v. Lyon*, 100 Wn.2d 409 (1983).
- **Survivorship disputes:** *Taufen v. Estate of Kirpes*, 155 Wn. App. 598 (2010); *Baker v. Leonard*, 120 Wn.2d 538 (1993).

**Secondary:** Northwest Justice Project, Small Estate Affidavit form (2025-08).
